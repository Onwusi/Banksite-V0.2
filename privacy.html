Privacy Policy

This page explains what account information NexaBank collects and how it's used.

Information we collect

When you register, we collect your name, email address, phone number, state, and country, along with a password and transaction PIN — both of which are stored as one-way hashes, never in plain text. Your activity within the product — deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and their statuses — is stored to display your account and transaction history back to you.

How information is used

Your information is used to operate your account: authenticating sign-in, displaying your balance and history, processing the requests you submit, and enabling account review where applicable. It is not sold, and it is not used for advertising.

Local storage on your device

A small amount of non-sensitive preference data — your light/dark theme choice and whether your balance is currently hidden — is stored in your browser's local storage so it persists between visits. Passwords and PINs are never stored this way.

Cookie policy

NexaBank does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Session authentication is handled through your browser's local storage rather than third-party tracking cookies.

Your data

You can view and update most of your account information directly from your Profile page at any time.
