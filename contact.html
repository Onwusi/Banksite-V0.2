Contact & Help

Have a question about your account, a transaction, or how NexaBank works? Here's how to get help.

Help Center

Most day-to-day questions are answered directly in the product:

  • Transfers, deposits, and withdrawals are reviewed before they're finalized — check the Transactions page for the current status of any request.
  • Transaction PIN issues can be resolved from Profile → Security, where you can set or change your PIN.
  • Account details like name, phone, state, and country can be updated from your Profile page.

Contact support

For anything not covered above, reach out and include your account number so we can look into it faster.
