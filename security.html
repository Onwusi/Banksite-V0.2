Security at NexaBank

Here's specifically how your account and data are protected — not marketing language, the actual mechanisms.

Authentication

Sign-in is handled by an authenticated session system. Passwords are never stored or visible to NexaBank in plain text, and every request to your account data is tied to your authenticated session.

Transaction PIN

Your 4-digit transaction PIN is stored as a salted hash — never in plain text, and never in your browser's local storage. Verifying a PIN happens entirely on the server; the PIN itself is never sent back to the browser.

Reviewed transactions

Deposits, withdrawals, and transfers are never applied to your balance the moment you submit them. Each request is created in a pending state and only affects your balance after it passes a server-side review step — your balance can't be changed by a request alone.

Access control

Administrative actions are restricted at the database level based on your account's role, not by anything the browser reports. A standard account cannot perform administrative actions, even by modifying requests directly.

Reporting an issue

If you believe you've found a security issue, please contact support and describe what you found.
